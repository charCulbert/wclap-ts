wclap-ts examples

Each page shows its own code at the bottom.

  • One plug-in: PICOH-101 with its own interface, a keyboard, MIDI and presets.
  • Chain builder: add, reorder and bypass plug-ins while it plays; a note sequencer into a synth into a delay.
  • A/B: two synths, every note on the same frame, switched without a click.
  • Mix: two latency-compensated tracks sending to a shared delay, from chains and Web Audio nodes.
  • Graph: the same mix as your own graph in one AudioWorklet (createHost, wclap-ts/worklet).
